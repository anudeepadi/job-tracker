### Skills Analysis for Audiologist Positions in Hilo, HI

#### 1. Complete List of Required and Preferred Skills
**Required Skills:**
- Licensed Audiologist
- Knowledge of Maryland CNC Test
- Knowledge of Puretone Audiometry
- Ability to analyze medical records
- Strong communication skills
- Customer service orientation

**Preferred Skills:**
- Experience with electronic medical records (EMR) systems
- Experience working with veterans
- Detail-oriented
- Team collaboration skills

#### 2. Skills Categorized by Type

**Technical Skills:**
- Audiological assessment techniques (Maryland CNC Test, Puretone Audiometry)
- Electronic Medical Records (EMR) software proficiency

**Soft Skills:**
- Communication skills
- Customer service orientation
- Attention to detail
- Team collaboration

**Domain Knowledge:**
- Understanding of veteran healthcare needs
- Knowledge of VA disability benefits process

**Certifications:**
- State licensure as an Audiologist

#### 4. Key Insights about Skill Gaps and Opportunities
- **Skill Gaps:** The most significant gap appears to be in specialized audiological assessment techniques and understanding of EMR systems which are critical for performing the job effectively.
- **Opportunities:** Candidates can enhance their profiles by gaining certifications in audiological assessments and EMR systems, which are increasingly sought after in the healthcare field, especially in roles focused on veteran care.

This comprehensive skills development roadmap provides clear pathways for candidates to acquire the necessary skills to transition into Audiologist roles while serving the Veteran community effectively.
